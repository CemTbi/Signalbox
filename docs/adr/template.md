# NNNN. Title in the form of a decision

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names or roles

## Context

What problem are we solving? Which forces matter: requirements, constraints, risks? Keep it factual.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly. Include the details someone needs to implement it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we must now do or watch.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative, and why we didn't choose it.
